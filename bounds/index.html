The Zhang–Kawazumi invariant

For every compact connected Riemann surface X of genus g ≥ 2:

φ(X)>g(g+2)−(2g+1)Hgg−1,Hg=∑k=1g1k

In genus two this gives φ(X) > ½. The argument uses the canonical Arakelov metric and positivity of a bilinear form.

For hyperelliptic X, the stronger bound is φ(X) > (g/2)(Hg−1).
