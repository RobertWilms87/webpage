I’m Robert Wilms. I study arithmetic geometry: heights, intersection numbers and the geometry hidden in algebra.

Postdoctoral researcher at Université de Caen Normandie.

Since 2025, I have been the principal investigator of the ERDF project MERCI (Mesure et recherche de la complexité par intersection), funded by Région Normandie.
